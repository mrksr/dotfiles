# Writing style

Applies to documents, READMEs, slides, and prose written for Markus, not to code identifiers.

- British English: customisation, generalisation, optimised, colour, behaviour, centre.
- Oxford comma in every list of three or more.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a comma, a colon, brackets, or "to" in ranges ("5 to 7", not "5-7" in prose).
- Avoid "Not X; Y" phrasing.
- Short and to the point: plain sentences, numbers with units, no filler. Cut a first draft by about half when it
  reads long.
- Avoid sentences that pad the length of the text by enumerating facts that are not strictly needed.
- Avoid adding sentences toward the end of a paragraph or below a plot or image that add random facts for completeness.
- Before delivering, grep for `—`, `–`, `\w+iz(e|ation)`, `color`, `center`, `behavior`, and check lists of three for
  the Oxford comma.
